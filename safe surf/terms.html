What the app does

Safe Surf hides words, phrases and the items that contain them on your screen, based on a list you choose. It does this using Android's Accessibility Service, which you turn on yourself, and with its built-in browser.

Filtering is automatic and not perfect. Free version and unlock

Safe Surf is free with a limited number of words. An optional one-time purchase through Google Play unlocks unlimited words and the parent PIN. It is not a subscription. Payments and refunds are handled under Google Play's terms and refund policies.

Parent PIN

If you forget your PIN, the only way to remove it is to clear the app's data, which also deletes your word list. We cannot recover a PIN for you.
