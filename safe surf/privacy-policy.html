Safe Surf – Privacy Policy

Published by Etzan Soft · Effective 28 October 2026

Safe Surf is an Android app that hides words, phrases and the items that contain them (such as headlines and video tiles) on your screen. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: Safe Surf has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send what it reads to us or to anyone else.

What the Accessibility Service reads

If you choose to turn it on, Safe Surf uses Android's Accessibility Service to read the text shown on your screen in other apps, such as browsers and YouTube. It compares that text with the words you chose, and covers matching items with a box. This happens on your device, in memory, only while the text is on screen. Safe Surf does not save, log, upload or share the text it reads. It does not tap, type or perform actions for you.

The service is off until you agree to the in-app disclosure and switch it on in Android Settings. You can switch it off at any time in Settings > Accessibility.

What is stored on your device

  • The list of words and phrases you add.
  • If you set a parent PIN, a salted hash of it (never the PIN itself).
  • Whether you accepted the disclosure and whether the unlock is active.

This stays on your device. If Android's automatic backup is on, Google may include the word list and PIN hash in your device backup under your Google account, according to Google's own terms. We do not have access to it.

The built-in browser

The Browser tab loads websites you open, like any browser. Safe Surf does not collect or keep a record of the pages you visit. Websites you visit may set cookies or receive requests as they normally would, under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

The optional unlock is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Payment details go to Google, not to us. Safe Surf only learns whether the purchase is active on your Google account.

Children

Safe Surf is a tool for adults, including parents who want to filter what a child sees. It is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information, because we do not collect it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Safe Surf, to remove everything stored on your device. Backups held by Google can be managed in your Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
