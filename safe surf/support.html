Safe Surf – Support

Need help? Email etzan.soft@gmail.com and tell us your phone model and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I make it work in other apps like YouTube and Chrome?

Open the Words tab, add your words, then tap "Enable system-wide filter". Read and accept the disclosure, then switch on Safe Surf in the Accessibility settings that open.

Why does Safe Surf need the Accessibility Service?

It is the only way Android lets an app read text on screen in other apps, so Safe Surf can find your words and cover them. Nothing it reads is saved or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

The switch is greyed out in Accessibility settings.

Open Android Settings > Apps > Safe Surf, tap the three dots at the top right, and choose "Allow restricted settings". Then try again.

A word was not hidden.

Safe Surf covers text that an app shows to Android. Images, and text some apps keep hidden from Android, cannot be filtered. Check that the word is spelled in your list as it appears, and that the service is switched on.

I forgot my parent PIN.

Clear the app's data in Android Settings > Apps > Safe Surf > Storage. This removes the PIN and your word list, and you can start again.

How do I restore my unlock on a new phone?

Install Safe Surf from Google Play with the same Google account you bought it with, and open the app. The unlock is checked automatically.

How do I turn the filter off?

Go to Android Settings > Accessibility > Safe Surf and switch it off.
