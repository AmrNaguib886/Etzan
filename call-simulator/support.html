Call Simulator (Fake Call) · Etzan Soft

Need help? Write to us at etzan.soft@gmail.com and tell us your phone model, your Android version and what happened. Below are answers to the most common questions.

The call didn't ring

  • Allow all three permissions when the app asks: Notifications, Alarms and reminders, and Full-screen notifications. The app asks for them one after another the first time you schedule a call.
  • Make sure the phone isn't in silent mode or Do Not Disturb. Just like a real call, a silenced phone won't ring.
  • Some phones limit apps in the background. In the phone's settings, open the app's battery options and choose "Unrestricted" or "No restrictions", and allow auto-start if your phone has that setting.

I paid but the app is still locked

  • Check that you are signed in to the same Google account you used to buy it, and that you have an internet connection.
  • Close the app completely and open it again. Purchases are checked with Google Play each time the app opens.
  • If it still doesn't unlock, email us the date of the purchase.

Refunds

Payments and refunds are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund from your Google Play order history, under Google's refund rules.

Does the app make real phone calls?

No. It only shows a simulated call on your own phone.

Which languages does it support?

English, Arabic, French, Spanish and German. The app follows your phone's language. On Android 13 and newer you can also set a language for this app only in the phone's settings.

What data does the app collect?

None. Everything stays on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.
