Privacy Policy

App: Call Simulator (Fake Call) for Android · Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how the Call Simulator (Fake Call) app ("the app") handles information. In short: the app works entirely on your phone, has no account system, and does not send your personal information to the developer or to any third party.

1. Who we are

The app is published by Etzan Soft ("the developer"). You can contact us at etzan.soft@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

Call Simulator (Fake Call) lets you schedule a simulated incoming call on your own phone. You choose a caller name and number, and a time. At that time the app shows a call screen and plays your phone's ringtone. No real phone call is made and no audio is recorded.

3. Information we handle

  • Caller name and number. You can type them or pick a contact using Android's built-in contact picker. The app only receives the single contact you choose. This information is used on your device to display the fake call screen and is not sent anywhere. The app does not request access to your full contact list.
  • App settings. Your chosen call screen style and whether you have unlocked the app are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only confirmation from Google Play that the unlock purchase was completed. We never see your payment card or other payment details.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not record audio, read your messages, or access your location.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications – to show the incoming call.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) – to start the call at the time you choose.
  • Full-screen notifications – to show the call screen on your lock screen.
  • Vibration – to vibrate like a real call.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores is kept on your phone. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to delete on our side.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above.
